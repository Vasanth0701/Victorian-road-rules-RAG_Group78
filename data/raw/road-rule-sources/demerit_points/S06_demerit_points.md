# Demerit points

Source ID: S06
Organisation: Transport Victoria
Source title: Demerit points
URL: https://transport.vic.gov.au/road-and-active-transport/road-rules-and-safety/demerit-points

## What are demerit points?

Demerit points are penalty points recorded against a driver's record when they commit certain driving offences.

The number of demerit points a driver can receive before reaching their limit depends on the type of licence or permit they hold.

## Demerit point limits

### Learner and probationary drivers

The following drivers have a limit of:

- 5 demerit points in any 12-month period, or
- 12 demerit points in any 3-year period

This applies to:
- learner permit holders
- P1 probationary licence holders
- P2 probationary licence holders
- overseas licence holders with no Victorian licence who are under 22 years old

### Full licence and other drivers

The following drivers have a limit of:

- 12 demerit points in any 3-year period

This applies to:
- full driver licence holders
- other licence categories listed by Transport Victoria
- overseas licence holders with no Victorian licence who are 22 years old or older

## Reaching the demerit point limit

If a driver reaches or exceeds their demerit point limit, action may be taken against their licence or learner permit.

Depending on the driver's circumstances, this may include:
- licence or learner permit suspension
- an extended demerit point period
- disqualification from driving

It is against the law to drive while a licence or learner permit is suspended or while the driver is disqualified.

## Active demerit points

Demerit points are recorded against a driver's driving record.

Drivers can check their current active demerit points using their myVicRoads account or the myVicRoads app.
