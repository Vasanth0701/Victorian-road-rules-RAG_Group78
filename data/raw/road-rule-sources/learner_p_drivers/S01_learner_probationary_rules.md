# Learner and probationary driver road rules

Source ID: S01
Organisation: Transport Victoria
Source title: Learner and probationary driver road rules
URL: https://transport.vic.gov.au/road-and-active-transport/road-rules-and-safety/learner-and-probationary-driver-road-rules

## Learner permit restrictions

As a learner driver, you must:
- carry your digital learner permit, learner permit card or receipt every time you drive
- drive with a supervising driver who has a full and current licence
- have a zero blood alcohol concentration (BAC) at all times when you drive
- not tow a trailer, caravan or another vehicle
- not operate portable devices in any way while driving, including for phone calls and navigation

## Supervising driver requirements

Learner drivers must have a supervising driver in the front passenger seat at all times while driving.

The supervising driver must have an Australian or overseas driver’s licence that:
- is current and full, not probationary
- allows them to drive the type of vehicle being driven by the learner

When supervising a learner driver, the supervising driver must:
- carry their digital driver licence or driver licence card
- have no more than 0.05 BAC for regular drivers
- have 0.00 BAC if they are an authorised driving instructor or have a Z condition on their licence
- complete the required supervising-driver and declaration sections of the learner driver’s logbook

## P1 licence restrictions

A P1 driver must:
- carry their digital driver licence or driver licence card when driving
- display red P plates visible from at least 20 metres away at the front and rear of the vehicle
- have a zero blood alcohol concentration (BAC)
- drive an automatic vehicle if an A condition applies to their licence

A P1 driver cannot:
- carry more than one peer passenger aged 16 to under 22, subject to listed exceptions
- operate portable devices while driving, including for phone calls and navigation
- tow another vehicle or trailer except in specified circumstances
- drive a probationary prohibited vehicle unless an exemption or other listed exception applies

## P2 licence restrictions

A P2 driver must:
- carry their digital or P2 licence card when driving
- display green P plates visible from at least 20 metres away at the front and rear of the vehicle
- have a zero blood alcohol concentration (BAC)
- drive an automatic vehicle if an A condition applies to their licence

A P2 driver cannot:
- operate portable devices while driving, including for phone calls and navigation
- drive a probationary prohibited vehicle unless an exemption or other listed exception applies
