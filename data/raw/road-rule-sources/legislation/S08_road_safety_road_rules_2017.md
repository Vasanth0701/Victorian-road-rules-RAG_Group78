# Road Safety Road Rules 2017

Source ID: S08
Organisation: Victorian Legislation
Source title: Road Safety Road Rules 2017
URL: https://www.legislation.vic.gov.au/in-force/statutory-rules/road-safety-road-rules-2017

## Source status

The Road Safety Road Rules 2017 are the official statutory road rules for Victoria.

The Victorian Legislation website lists the current in-force version as effective from 18 February 2026.

## Purpose in this knowledge base

This source is used as the primary legal reference for Victorian road rules.

It can be used to verify rules described in explanatory sources from Transport Victoria and VicRoads.

The legislation covers areas including:
- speed limits
- traffic signs and road markings
- intersections
- giving way
- overtaking
- stopping and parking
- pedestrians
- cyclists
- use of lights and warning devices
- road-user behaviour
- other traffic and road-safety requirements

## Use for RAG evaluation

Where a Transport Victoria or VicRoads page provides a simplified explanation of a road rule, the relevant provision in the Road Safety Road Rules 2017 can be used as the authoritative legal reference.

For questions requiring a precise legal rule, the relevant rule number and legislative text should be retrieved from the current in-force version.
