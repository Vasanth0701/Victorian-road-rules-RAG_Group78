# Device rules for new and young drivers and motorcyclists

Source ID: S02
Organisation: Transport Victoria
Source title: Device rules for new and young drivers and motorcyclists
URL: https://transport.vic.gov.au/road-and-active-transport/road-rules-and-safety/mobile-phones-and-devices/device-rules-for-new-and-young-drivers-and-motorcyclists

## Who these rules apply to

These rules apply to:
- learner drivers
- P1 drivers
- P2 drivers
- motorcyclists who hold an E (restricted) motorcycle licence

## General rules

A learner, P1 or P2 driver must not use a mobile phone or portable device for any function while driving unless they pull over and park.

While driving, they must not:
- use or touch a mobile phone or portable, wearable or mounted device, including for phone calls or navigation
- use voice controls to operate a device
- enter information, text, numbers or symbols
- scroll through content
- view messages, social media, emails or photos
- watch videos, play games or take video calls
- place a device on their lap or body, except in a pocket or holding pouch
- pass a device to a passenger or take one from a passenger

## Portable devices

Portable devices include loose or unmounted:
- mobile phones
- tablets
- laptops
- media players
- game consoles

A driver must not:
- touch a portable device, whether it is turned on or off
- use a portable device, including through voice controls
- look at another person's device screen
- have ongoing activity on a portable device, even if navigation or audio was set up before driving
- keep a portable device on their lap or body, except in a pocket or holding pouch

To operate a portable device, the driver must pull over and park.

## Mounted devices and in-built vehicle systems

A mounted mobile phone or tablet must be secured using a commercially designed holder.

A learner, P1 or P2 driver must not:
- scroll or enter information on a mounted or in-built device
- use voice controls
- use the device for messages, social media, emails or websites
- use the device for video calls

They may:
- use a mounted device for navigation or audio if it is set up before the journey starts
- briefly touch an in-built vehicle device to adjust navigation, climate controls or audio functions

A mounted device must not be touched during the drive, including while stopped at traffic lights. The driver must pull over and park to change the address or audio settings.

## Wearable devices

Wearable devices include:
- smartwatches
- smart glasses
- wearable head-up displays

A driver must not:
- touch a wearable device
- use voice controls to operate it
- look at the screen of a wearable device being used by another person

Audio may continue if it was set up before driving. The driver must pull over and park before touching the device or changing its settings.

## Motorbike helmet devices

Motorbike helmet devices include:
- communicators
- head-up displays
- cameras
- built-in or secured mobile phones

Learner and probationary motorcyclists may use these devices for navigation or audio only if the function is set up before riding.

They must not touch the device or use voice controls while riding. Any changes must be made after pulling over and parking.
