# Device rules for drivers with a full licence

Source ID: S03
Organisation: Transport Victoria
Source title: Device rules for drivers with a full licence
URL: https://transport.vic.gov.au/road-and-active-transport/road-rules-and-safety/mobile-phones-and-devices/device-rules-for-drivers-with-a-full-licence

## General rules for all devices

Drivers with a full licence can use a mobile phone or device to:
- make or receive a phone call
- use audio and music functions
- use GPS navigation
- use in-built driver assistance or vehicle safety features

The device must be properly mounted or built into the vehicle.

Fully licensed drivers must not touch or use an unmounted mobile phone or device while driving or riding.

While driving, riding or operating a vehicle, a driver must not:
- enter information, text, numbers or symbols, unless using voice control
- scroll through content such as text messages, social media or music playlists
- view text messages, social media, emails, websites or photos
- watch videos, play games or take video calls

These rules apply to portable, wearable, mounted and in-built devices.

## Portable devices

Portable devices include unmounted or loose:
- mobile phones
- tablets
- laptops
- media players
- game consoles

While driving, a fully licensed driver must not:
- touch a portable device, whether it is on or off
- operate it using voice
- look at the screen of a device being used by another person
- place the device on their lap or body, except in a pocket or attached pouch
- pass a device to a passenger or receive one from a passenger

A passenger must not pass a portable device to the driver.

A fully licensed driver may:
- connect a portable device to the vehicle's wireless technology before driving and place the device out of sight and reach
- use a portable device to pay at a drive-through
- use a CB radio or another two-way radio

## Mounted devices and in-built vehicle systems

A mobile phone or tablet is considered mounted when it is secured using a commercially designed and manufactured holder.

Mounted devices and in-built vehicle systems may be used for functions such as music and maps, provided the driver does not enter text, scroll, view images or play videos.

While driving, the driver must not:
- use the device for video calls

The driver may briefly touch a mounted or in-built device to:
- initiate, accept or reject an audio call
- play or stream audio
- adjust volume
- use navigation
- use driver-assistance or vehicle-operation functions
- use functions that monitor driver behaviour or condition
- perform a professional driving task

## Wearable devices

Wearable devices include:
- smartwatches
- smart glasses
- wearable head-up displays

While driving, a driver must not:
- touch the wearable device, except for incidental contact caused by wearing it
- use it for video calls
- use its navigation function
- use functions that monitor driver behaviour or condition
- perform a professional driving task using it

Using voice controls, the driver may:
- initiate, accept or reject an audio call
- play or stream audio
- adjust volume

## Motorbike helmet devices

Motorbike helmet devices include:
- communicators
- head-up displays
- cameras
- in-built or secured mobile phones

A rider may briefly touch the device or use voice commands to:
- initiate, accept or reject an audio call
- play or stream audio
- adjust volume
- use driver-assistance functions
- use navigation
- use functions that monitor rider behaviour or condition
- perform a professional driving task

Earbuds may also be used if the phone is mounted and used hands-free.
