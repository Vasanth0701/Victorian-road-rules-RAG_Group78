# Driving with your overseas licence in Victoria

Source ID: S07
Organisation: VicRoads
Source title: Driving with your overseas licence in Victoria
URL: https://www.vicroads.vic.gov.au/licences/convert-your-licence/driving-with-your-overseas-licence

## When an overseas licence can be used

A person can use their overseas licence to drive in Victoria if:
- the licence is current
- the licence is valid for the type of vehicle being driven
- they plan to live and drive in Victoria for less than 6 months

The 6-month period starts from the day the person starts living in Victoria.

Leaving Victoria and returning does not restart the 6-month period.

If a person wants to drive in Victoria for more than 6 months, they must convert their overseas licence to a Victorian licence.

## When an overseas licence cannot be used

A person cannot use their overseas licence to drive in Victoria if:
- they have been disqualified or suspended from driving or obtaining a licence or permit in any country
- they have failed a driving test in Victoria
- they already hold an Australian driver licence or permit from another state or territory

## What to carry while driving

Drivers should carry their overseas licence when driving in Victoria.

If the overseas licence is not in English, the driver must also carry either:
- an English translation of the licence, or
- an international driving permit in English

## Translation requirements

If an overseas licence is not in English, it must be supported by an appropriate English translation or international driving permit.

For licence conversion, VicRoads states that translations may need to be completed by a NAATI-accredited translator or the relevant country's consulate in Australia.

## Converting to a Victorian licence

A person who moves to Victoria and intends to live there for more than 6 months must convert their overseas driver licence or learner permit to a Victorian licence.

The exact conversion requirements depend on factors such as:
- the type of licence
- the person's age
- how long they have held the overseas licence
- the country or jurisdiction where the licence was issued

## Victorian road rules

Anyone driving with an overseas licence in Victoria must understand and follow Victorian road rules.
