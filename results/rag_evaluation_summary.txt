VICTORIAN ROAD RULES RAG ASSISTANT
FINAL RAG EVALUATION SUMMARY

1. Evaluation Setup

The final RAG system was evaluated using 10 ground-truth Victorian road-rule questions (Q01-Q10) and 3 additional out-of-scope questions.

The ground-truth evaluation assessed:
- Answer correctness and relevance
- Support from retrieved context
- Retrieval of the expected source
- Expected source ranking
- Incorrect or unsupported information
- Completeness of generated answers

The out-of-scope evaluation assessed whether the system correctly refused to answer questions that were not supported by the Victorian road-rules knowledge base.

2. Retrieval Evaluation

Existing retrieval evaluation results for the 10 ground-truth questions were:

BM25:
- Hit@1: 8/10 (80.0%)
- Hit@3: 10/10 (100.0%)
- Hit@5: 10/10 (100.0%)

Dense FAISS:
- Hit@1: 9/10 (90.0%)
- Hit@3: 10/10 (100.0%)
- Hit@5: 10/10 (100.0%)

Dense FAISS achieved a higher Hit@1 result than BM25 (90% compared with 80%). Both approaches achieved 100% Hit@3 and Hit@5.

3. End-to-End RAG Evaluation

All 10 ground-truth questions received answers that were judged correct and relevant to the question.

- Correct/relevant answers: 10/10 (100%)
- Answers supported by retrieved context: 10/10 (100%)
- Expected source retrieved within Top-3: 10/10 (100%)
- Expected source at Rank 1: 9/10 (90%)

For Q03, the expected source was successfully retrieved but appeared at Rank 3 rather than Rank 1.

Q06 produced the correct demerit-point limits, although the additional explanatory sentence contained a minor clarity issue.

Q08 produced correct P1 restriction information but was incomplete compared with the ground truth. It omitted the 0.00 BAC requirement, the requirement to carry the licence, and the automatic-transmission condition.

Q09 also produced correct information but was incomplete compared with the ground truth. It omitted the requirement for the supervisor to sit in the front passenger seat and to hold a current full licence valid for the relevant vehicle type.

These results show that successful retrieval does not always guarantee a fully complete generated answer, particularly for broad questions requiring several rules to be combined.

4. Out-of-Scope Evaluation

Three out-of-scope questions were tested:
- Learner driver rules in New South Wales
- Car insurance cost in Victoria
- Fastest driving route from Melbourne to Sydney

The system correctly identified insufficient information for all three questions and did not invent unsupported answers.

- Correctly unanswered: 3/3 (100%)
- Unsupported/hallucinated out-of-scope answers: 0/3 (0%)

5. Key Findings

The evaluation shows strong retrieval performance at Top-3 and strong end-to-end answer correctness on the ground-truth test set.

Dense FAISS performed better than BM25 at Hit@1, while both methods achieved complete source coverage by Top-3.

The RAG system was also effective at handling the tested out-of-scope questions, correctly declining all three rather than generating unsupported information.

The main observed limitation was answer completeness. Q08 and Q09 retrieved the correct source at Rank 1 but omitted some ground-truth details from the generated response. Q06 also demonstrated a minor clarity issue in additional generated explanation.

Overall, the evaluation indicates that the system can retrieve relevant Victorian road-rule information, generate grounded answers, and appropriately decline the tested unsupported questions, while generation completeness remains an area for improvement.
